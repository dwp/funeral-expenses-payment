{# -----------------MASTER LIST----------------

What we need

Before we can process your claim we need a copy of:

  • the funeral bill/signed contract/funeral bill or signed contract/the bill from the local authority and any other services used for the funeral
  • the receipt for flowers or wreath, service sheet, [description of other cost]
  • the receipts for your travel costs
  • any final statements from the accounts of the person who died
  • any documents from the pre-paid funeral plan provider that shows what they are paying for

You will need to check if the person who died had any bank accounts, ISAs, cash, lump sum pension payments, pre-paid funeral plans, burial society payments, War Pension Funeral Grants, other money.

We cannot pay for any costs that are covered by a pre-paid funeral plan.

How to send it

You can send us what we need by email or post.

You’ll need to include your full name, address and National Insurance number with anything you send, so we can match it to your claim.

Email

funeral.bills@dwp.gov.uk

Post

Freepost DWP, Funeral Payments

What we will do next

We will contact the person:

  • responsible for the financial affairs of the person who died
  • applying for probate, letters of administration or confirmation to ask if there is any money available from the estate

We will contact the [FD] for a copy of the funeral bill. If you can get a copy from them, or ask them to send it to us, we may be able to process your claim faster.
